Paper, ink, pink & a little yellow

The rules behind Susana Sánchez Restrepo's portfolio: the same sidebar-and-sections site, systematised so every size, space and colour has one reason to exist. Accessible first — WCAG 2.2 AA is the floor, not the goal.

1 · Principles

01Quiet by defaultInk on warm paper does the work. Colour marks meaning — it never decorates.
02Summary firstEvery section opens with an "In short" a recruiter can read in ten seconds. Depth lives in collapsibles.
03One token per jobNo near-duplicate sizes or spaces. If a value isn't in this file, it doesn't ship.
04Accessible is the styleLarge body text, 44px targets, visible focus, real headings — the same values Bloom holds itself to.

3 · The yellow rule

Yellow appears in exactly three places, each answering a question a visitor has. It is always a background under ink, never text on paper (yellow text fails contrast in light mode).

4 · Typography

Newsreader (display and headings, weight 400 only — a lower-contrast serif than a Didone, easier on the eye) and Source Sans 3 (everything else, 400 / 600). Eight sizes, down from the fourteen on the current site.

  • Body line length capped at 68ch; text-wrap: pretty on all paragraphs.
  • Italic serif is reserved for quotations.
  • Uppercase only for kickers and group labels (≤ 4 words), always tracked +0.08em.
  • Sentence case for every heading — no Title Case.

8 · Writing rules

Do
  • First person, active voice, concrete verbs: built, shipped, led.
  • Real numbers with their context.
  • Lead each section with the outcome, then the how.
  • Keep French quotes in French.
Don't
  • Repeat the same idea across sections — link to it instead.
  • Paragraphs over 90 words.
  • Telling the same story twice. Each fact lives in one section; other sections link to it.
  • Em dashes. Use a comma, a colon or a new sentence so lines read fluently.
  • Stats without a source, or emoji.
  • "Passionate", "innovative", "cutting-edge".
